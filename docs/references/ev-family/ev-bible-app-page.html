EV Bible

Version 1.0

This application is for Escape Velocity plugin developers. It contains the 'Bibles' for EV/Override and EV Nova.

The available Bibles are displayed in a table to the right of where the data from the bible will be shown. You can click once on any of the 'Topics' and it will be displayed in the text view. When you click on a topic the title of that topic along with the version of the game it goes with, will be set as the title of the window. This can be useful when you have multiple Bibles open (you can achieve this by using the 'New' command, or 'Command-N') so you can easily distinguish between the windows. They also appear in the 'Window' menu and can be gotten to from there.

With this application one can store 'notes' for later reference. You type in the field below the Bible page and the text you have typed gets saved as soon as you switch to some other part of the app. For example: You type in something and then you go to a new topic, the notes were just saved and you don't have to worry about them. Also, they are loaded automatically when you switch to their topic.
